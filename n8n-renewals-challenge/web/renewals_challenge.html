Part 3

From approved quote to signature, every morning

I extended my own n8n workflow, Daily renewal alerts (built before this challenge), so it covers the part of the renewal that eats the most time once a quote exists: emailing the customer, sending the order form, updating the CRM and chasing the signature. At 221 renewals a quarter, that's where days slip.

AI AI drafts the email   ✓ a person approves   DB reads or writes CRM / CPQ

What it does, step by step

  1. Every morning at 8:00 it reads the renewals due within 90 days that aren't signed yet, and pulls the quotes already approved in the CPQ.
  2. It matches each renewal to its approved quote, so every email uses the exact offer, order form and quote number the customer will sign.
  3. It routes each renewal to one of five paths:
    • On track: AI drafts the renewal email around the approved offer → I approve it → it goes out with the order form for e-signature.
    • Upsell (usage above the plan): AI drafts the renewal email and a separate upsell email → I approve both → both go out.
    • Chase signature: 3 and 7 days after sending, if it isn't signed, AI drafts a short follow-up → I approve → it goes out.
    • Risk (red health, usage dropping): no customer email. The CSM gets an alert to act first.
    • No quote yet: I get a reminder to finish the quote in the CPQ before the deadline slips.
  4. Nothing reaches a customer without my click. Each draft arrives in my inbox with Approve and Reject buttons (Gmail send-and-wait), and the workflow waits.
  5. After sending, it writes back to the CRM: status Quoted and the date sent. That date starts the signature chase.

Why this design

  • Numbers come from the approved quote, never from the AI. It copies the offer word for word, so pricing and terms stay in the CRM and CPQ.
  • AI does the writing, where it saves the most time. A person keeps every decision that reaches a customer.
  • Same principles as the renewal quotation agent I built at my current company: one job per step, a source of truth, and autonomy earned with a track record.

How I'd know it works

  • Manual renewals with the quote out by day 60: 1 of 7 → 100%
  • Approved quote to email sent: same day
  • Order forms signed within 7 days of sending: tracked weekly
  • AI drafts approved without edits: tracked. Once it's consistently high, standard renewals can go out without the approval click.

Tested on five accounts, one per path (Northwind, Kestrel, Halden, Tessera, Marlowe), with no real emails sent. In the prototype, Google Sheet tabs stand in for Salesforce and the CPQ; the workflow JSON is attached and can be imported.
