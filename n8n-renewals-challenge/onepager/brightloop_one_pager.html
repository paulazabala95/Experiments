Brightloop renewal: negotiation plan

Enterprise plan · €36,000/yr · renews 14 Dec 2026 · champion Sam (automation engineer) · procurement Priya · prepared Tue 20 Oct

Situation

  • Asks: 25% off, net 60, no auto-renewal, 3-month termination for convenience, smaller plan.
  • Real drivers: a real budget cut; procurement needs a visible win; two slow support escalations.
  • Small dip: executions down only 10%. Sam values the product.
  • Value first, with the CSM: before any price, we show what n8n runs for them (workflows, executions, hours saved) and what replacing it would cost. Concessions follow value, never replace it.
  • Their alternative: the free self-hosted Community edition. It loses SSO, environments/Git, log streaming and SLA support, and adds hosting work.

Walk-away position

  • Never: a termination-for-convenience right, or removing the auto-renewal clause.
  • Current plan floor: no more than 12% total discount vs list. That's a 2-year total of at least €64,944 (Finance's limit).
  • Below that: downsize at list price. Honest contraction beats a discount that resets the price for 220 other renewals.
  • VP escalation only for something new: a 3-year term on the current plan, or a public case study.
In reserve (ramp only): a downgrade buffer of up to 10% of the execution allowance at the yr-2 anniversary, 60 days' notice, pre-approved by the VP. Used only if Priya pushes again for an exit right. Even if used in full, they pay 12.3% less than the list path over 2 years.
