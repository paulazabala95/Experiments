# Part 2: Brightloop negotiation

## A. My reply to Sam (sent Tue 20 Oct)

> **Subject:** Brightloop renewal: two ways to make it fit your budget
>
> Hi Sam,
>
> Thank you for being this open about the budget pressure. That makes it much easier to find something that works.
>
> First, the two support escalations this year. They took longer to resolve than they should have, and I'm sorry. I've asked our support lead to review both, and I'll bring what we find and what we're changing to our call.
>
> On procurement's four points: I can't agree to them exactly as written. A 25% discount, removing auto-renewal and a termination-for-convenience right are outside what we offer on any Enterprise renewal. But I don't think we need them to reach a number your budget can carry. I'd like to walk you and Priya through two options:
>
> - **Keep your current plan, with a lower cost this year.** A multi-year structure where this year costs less than today, and over two years you pay no more than you do now.
> - **Right-size and lock the price.** We look at your actual execution data together, set your plan to what you really use, and lock that price for two or three years.
>
> Both would come with our highest support tier.
>
> Could we set up 30 minutes with you and Priya on Thursday or Friday? It would help if I came in knowing:
> 1. the budget figure you need to land on, and whether the pressure is on this year's spend or on cash;
> 2. who signs, and by when;
> 3. what "evaluate alternatives" means in practice (self-hosting? another vendor?).
>
> Your renewal date is 14 December, so if we agree terms by the end of November there is plenty of time for paperwork.
>
> Best,
> Paula
> Renewals, n8n

*Why this email:*
- It opens with the support problem, which is the real emotional issue.
- It says no to the four asks once, clearly, without arguing each one in writing.
- It offers two directions, not prices, so the numbers are discussed live with the decision makers.

## B. One-page negotiation plan

*The printable one-page version is `onepager/brightloop_one_pager.pdf`. The detail is below.*

**Where we start**
- Today's price is **€36,000**. The list path, paid annually with the 5% uplift, is **€37,800 in year 1 and €39,690 in year 2**.
- Sam's ask of €27,000 is 28.6% below list, which would need VP CS.
- Procurement also wants net 60, no auto-renewal and a 3-month termination right.

**What's really going on**
- **The budget cut is real** (CSM). The 10% drop in executions is small.
- **Procurement** is new and needs a visible win.
- **Sam values the product.** The two slow escalations are behind "evaluate alternatives".
- **For n8n, the realistic alternative is the free self-hosted Community edition.** Leaving Enterprise means losing SSO, environments and Git version control, log streaming, external secrets and SLA support, and taking on hosting and DevOps work themselves.

### The two options (procurement chooses)

| | **1. Ramp: keep the plan, pay annually** (target) | **2. Downsize + lock: pay upfront** (budget option) |
|---|---|---|
| Plan | Current Enterprise plan | Enterprise with a lower execution allowance, sized to real usage (price **assumed** ~20% lower: €28,800) |
| Price | Year 1 **€34,020** (−10%) · Year 2 **€37,706** (indexed, −5%) | **€28,800 a year, locked** for 2 or 3 years |
| Contract value | **€71,726**: less than today's €72,000 over two years | €57,600 (2 years) · €86,400 (3 years) |
| Concession vs list path | 7.4% discount | List price; the waived uplift = 2.4% (2 years) / 4.8% (3 years) |
| Payment | Annual, net 30 or 45 | **Upfront**, net 60 |
| Approval | Finance (6–12% band) | Finance (net 60; confirm the 3-year lock) |
| Flexibility | No downgrade during the term. Downgrade buffer held in reserve (below). | No downgrade during the term |
| Both include | Top support tier · auto-renewal clause applying only after the committed term, plus a 90-day reminder from us · no termination right | |

**Which one to lead with depends on one discovery answer: is their problem cash, or this year's cost line?**
- **Cash is tight, or the pressure is temporary:** lead with the **ramp**.
- **They really use less and have cash now:** lead with **downsize + lock**.

The ramp is the better outcome for n8n: they keep the full plan, and ARR is above today's price by year 2.

**Held in reserve: the downgrade buffer (ramp only).** At the year-2 anniversary they may reduce the execution allowance by up to 10%, with 60 days' notice. VP CS approves it before the call. I only use it if Priya pushes again for an exit right, as the answer to the risk the termination clause was meant to cover. Even if it's used in full, they pay 12.3% less than the list path over two years. Of that, 7.4% is discount and the rest is a smaller plan.

**Walk-away**
- **Never:** a termination-for-convenience right, or removing the auto-renewal clause.
- **On the current plan:** no more than **12% total discount against the list path**. That's a 2-year total of at least €68,191, Finance's limit.
- **If they need less than that:** downsize at list price. I'd rather book honest contraction than a discount that resets the price for 220 other renewals.
- **Escalate to the VP only** if Brightloop brings something new, such as a 3-year term on the current plan or a public case study.

**What I give, and what I ask for each time ("if… then…")**

| I give | Only if |
|---|---|
| Year-1 relief (ramp) | 2-year commitment, signed by 30 Nov |
| Price lock from year 2 | Payment upfront |
| Net 60 | Payment upfront |
| Top support tier | Multi-year commitment, plus a joint support review |
| Downgrade buffer | Their termination-right demand is dropped |
| Auto-renewal applying only after the term + 90-day reminder | The clause stays in the order form |
| Free: support root-cause review, exec sponsor call, CSM usage-optimisation session | |

**What I ask for in return overall:** a multi-year term, signature by **30 November** (offers valid until then), a reference or case study if possible, and one named signatory.

**Who I involve, before the call**
- **Finance:** pre-approve the ramp (7.4%), net 60 with upfront payment, the 3-year lock and room up to 12%.
- **VP CS:** pre-approve the downgrade buffer, and a heads-up that €36k is at risk.
- **Legal:**
  - wording for the auto-renewal clause after the term;
  - the buffer clause;
  - the termination-right decline.
- **CSM:** usage data to size the lower allowance; owns Sam and the success plan.
- **Support lead:** root-cause review of the two escalations by 30 Oct; confirm the top support tier and whether it's a paid add-on.
- **My manager:** a weekly status update.

## C. Call playbook (15 minutes, I keep time)

| Min | Segment | What I do |
|---|---|---|
| 0–1 | Open | Agenda, time check, confirm who decides and who signs |
| 1–4 | Discovery (Sam) | Budget figure; **cash or this year's cost?**; fiscal year; why executions fell 10% (seasonal, a project ended, moving work elsewhere) |
| 4–6 | Support | Own the two escalations, share the remediation plan and the top support tier |
| 6–10 | Options (Priya joins) | Show both options side by side; lead with the one discovery points to |
| 10–13 | Trade | Only "if… then…". One concession for each thing I get back. Never split the difference. |
| 13–15 | Close | Summarise, name an owner for each open item, set the date the order form goes out, confirm signature by 30 Nov |

### Surprises I expect, and how I'd answer

| They say | I answer |
|---|---|
| "We have a competitor quote 40% cheaper." | "Can you share what's in scope? Most quotes leave out migration, rebuilding the workflows, and Enterprise controls." I don't match the price. I offer the ramp or the downsize. |
| "We'll just self-host the free version." | It's a real option. I walk through what they'd lose (SSO, environments, Git, log streaming, SLA support) and the DevOps cost, and offer a technical session with Sam. |
| "Net 60 is company policy." | "I can do net 60 when the contract is paid upfront: that's the downsize + lock option." |
| "We want the price lock but we pay annually." | "The lock comes with upfront payment. Paying annually, the ramp gives you this year's relief instead." |
| "All four points, or no deal." | "I can't do the exit clause or remove auto-renewal for anyone. What problem are they solving for you?" Then I solve that problem: the buffer, the reminder, and the clause applying only after the term. |
| "Fine, 3 years for 25% off." | "3 years changes the picture, and I'll take it to my VP. I can't commit to 25%." I come back within 48 hours. |
| Priya: "Our budget is €30k." | That fits downsize + lock (€28.8k). If they need the full plan, the ramp's year 1 is €34k, and I check whether a 12% year 1 (still within Finance's limit) closes the gap. |
| "Sam is leaving" / "Sam isn't the decision maker" | Ask for an intro to the budget owner; bring in the CSM and an exec sponsor. |
| "Sign today, final offer." | I only offer what's pre-approved. Anything else, I answer within 24 hours. |
| "Give us service credits for the escalations." | Credits are a discount by another name. I offer the top support tier, the remediation plan and a quarterly support review. |
| "Just remove the 5% uplift on a 1-year deal." | That's a discount under our policy. Two years with the ramp gets you more than that. |
| Silence until 14 Dec | The offers expire on 30 Nov. If nothing is signed by 1 Dec, I escalate exec to exec. |

**Debrief line:** "I moved the conversation from *discount* to *structure*: they chose between keeping the plan with a ramp, or a smaller plan with a locked price. Every concession was traded, and nothing reset the price."
